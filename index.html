Ganit & Vigyan Quick Sutra

📐 Ganit (Beejganit & Trikonmiti)

• aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ | a⁰ = 1 | a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ
• (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b² | (a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
• a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b) | (x+a)(x+b) = x² + (a+b)x + ab
• sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 | 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ | 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
• SI = (P×R×T)/100 | CI A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ

⚡ Bhautiki (Physics SI & Sutra)

• Chaal = Duri/Samay (m/s) | Twaran a = (v−u)/t (m/s²)
• Gati: v = u + at | s = ut + ½at² | v² = u² + 2as
• Bal F = m × a (Newton) | Samveg p = m × v (kg·m/s)
• Gurutvakarshan F = G·m₁m₂/r² (G = 6.67×10⁻¹¹)
• Karya W = F×s (Joule) | Shakti P = W/t (Watt) | KE = ½mv² | PE = mgh
